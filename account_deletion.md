# Carvia - Delete Your Account and Data

App: Carvia (Android package `com.sn.carvia`) by Soft Nova
Contact: softnova.global@gmail.com

## Delete your account in the app (fastest)

1. Open Carvia and go to **Settings > Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm your identity again (Google account or your password).
4. Choose whether the data stored on this phone should also be deleted.

## What is deleted

- Your cloud data in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore: vehicles, fuel and maintenance records, shortcuts, favorites, email and display name.
- Your Access time record.
- Your Firebase Authentication account.
- Data on your phone, if you choose this option. You can also clear it by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

## What may remain

- A welcome marker (an anti-abuse record containing your email address and account ID) is kept so that the one-time welcome Access time cannot be claimed again. You can ask us to review it by emailing us.
- Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics expire automatically after about 90 days.
- Firebase and Google may take a short technical time to remove copies from their own systems.

## If you cannot use the app

Email softnova.global@gmail.com from the email address used for your account with the subject "Carvia account deletion". We may ask you to verify that the account is yours. We will reply within 30 days.

Guest users: no account is created, your data is on your device. To remove the anonymous Access time record of a guest, email us with the subject "Carvia guest data deletion".
