## 3. Driving safety
Do not use Carvia in a way that distracts you from driving. Obey traffic laws. Some features can be limited while the vehicle is moving. The car web browser and screen sharing have a setting: with "Only when stopped", Carvia dims the content and ignores touch input while the phone's GPS speed shows that the vehicle is moving; with "Always available", Carvia applies no such limit and you alone are responsible for using these features safely. The limit depends on location permission and a working GPS signal: if the speed is unknown, Carvia cannot detect movement and does not restrict the content, so it is not a guarantee that a feature is unavailable while driving. Carvia reads the speed of your phone only, not of your vehicle. Use the browser and screen sharing only when it is safe and legal to do so, preferably while parked. You can stop screen sharing at any time from the notification. Station, map and other information may be incomplete or out of date (it comes from third-party open data). You use it at your own risk and must verify what matters. In-app route guidance to a station (where available) is advisory only: routes and instructions may be wrong, out of date or unavailable, do not include live traffic, and may not reflect road closures or restrictions. Always follow traffic signs, road conditions and the law, and do not interact with the screen while driving. When in-app guidance is not available, Carvia offers to open another navigation app instead. Entries you make (fuel, maintenance, inspection dates) are your own records and not professional advice.
